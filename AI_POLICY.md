# AI-assisted contribution policy

AI-assisted and AI-generated contributions are allowed in this fork. Review is
based on the quality of the submitted work, not the tools used to produce it.

Contributors must:

1. Understand and take responsibility for every submitted change.
2. Keep changes focused and explain their behavior in plain language.
3. Test the work appropriately and report results honestly.
4. Verify generated claims, APIs, dependencies, licenses, and attribution.
5. Never submit secrets, private data, fabricated evidence, or copied work
   without a compatible license.
6. Respond to review feedback and revise or remove work that does not meet the
   repository's quality bar.

Disclosure of routine AI assistance is welcome but not mandatory. Disclosure is
required when it is materially relevant to licensing, attribution, privacy, or
the reviewer's ability to assess the change.

The repository owner,
[@aytekaksu](https://github.com/aytekaksu), has final approval and merge
authority.
